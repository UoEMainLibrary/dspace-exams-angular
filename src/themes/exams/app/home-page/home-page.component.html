Exam Papers Online provides access for staff and students of the University of Edinburgh to the collected degree examination papers of the University from 2004/05 onwards. They are for use by students as a study aid.

Search Tips

If you can't find the paper you are looking for from a title search, try:

  • entering the course code. Course codes can change over time. Click the 'All papers from this course title' link in your search results to get full results for the course title.
  • putting quotation marks (“…”) around your search term will return the most accurate results.
  • remove any punctuation, e.g. :,?

If your search results don't include a particular academic year, then the course may not have been delivered that year

Marking guides and sample solutions

You won’t find marking guides or sample solutions on this site. Please contact your course organiser or the course secretary to find out if any have been created for your course and how to access them.

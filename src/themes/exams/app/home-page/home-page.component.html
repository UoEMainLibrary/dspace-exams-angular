Exam Papers Online provides access for staff and students of the University of Edinburgh to the collected degree examination papers of the University from 2004/05 onwards. They are for use by students as a study aid.

If you cannot find the paper you are looking for from a title search:

  • try entering the course code. Course codes can change over time. Click the 'All papers from this course title' link in your search results to get full results for the course title.
  • try putting quotation marks (“…”) around your search term will return the most accurate results.
  • remove any punctuation, e.g. :,?

If an academic year is not listed in your search results for a course, then it is likely that the course was not delivered in that year.

Marking guides and sample solutions are not available here Please contact your course organiser or the course secretary to find out if any have been created for your course and how to access them.

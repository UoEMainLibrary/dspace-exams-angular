Exam Papers Online provides access for staff and students of the University of Edinburgh to the collected degree examination papers of the University from 2004/05 onwards. They may be used by students as a study aid only.

These pages are updated periodically as papers become available. If you cannot find what you are looking for, try visiting again at a later date or email exam.papers@ed.ac.uk.

Marking Guides and Sample Solutions

Please contact your course organiser or the course secretary to find out if any have been created for your course and how to access them.

Search Tips
  • If you cannot find the paper you are looking for from a title search, try entering the course code. Course codes can change over time. Click the 'All papers from this course title' link in your search results to get full results for the course title.
  • Putting quotation marks (“…”) around your search term will return the most accurate results.
  • If searching by course title, remove any punctuation, e.g. :,?
  • If an academic year is not listed in your search results for a course, then it is likely that the course was not delivered in that year.
